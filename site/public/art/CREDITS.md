# Art credits

The pictures in this folder are **generated by AI**. They make up "Mapless", the invented series the showcase
uses as demo art: volumes 1 and 2, two pages of chapter 1, and a portrait. Characters: Suzu (an apprentice
cartographer), Kuro (an ink dragon) and the Eraser (a white knight). The series, its characters and its
title were made for this repository; they do not come from an existing work.

Sources (kept unchanged in `site/art-source/`):

- `suzu-portrait`: Suzu laughs, holding up a map, Kuro on her head.
- `cover-1`: cover of volume 1, Suzu and Kuro above the clouds.
- `cover-2`: cover of volume 2, the Eraser.
- `page-1-bridge`: the ink bridge page.
- `page-2-lighthouse`: the lighthouse page.

Every other file is a crop of one of these five (the list and the boxes are in `scripts/art-images.py`),
resized to several widths and saved as WebP. The pictures are already inked: they are never toned again,
except in the TonedImage demo, whose subject is toning a picture.

The registry items ship none of these files: components and blocks take picture URLs as props.
