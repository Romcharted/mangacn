# Credits

Panels cut from **Little Nemo in Slumberland**, Sunday page of 27 May 1906, by **Winsor McCay** (1869–1934),
published by the New York Herald. Public domain (published in 1906; the author died in 1934).

Source scan: [File:Little Nemo 1906-05-27.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Little_Nemo_1906-05-27.jpg),
Wikimedia Commons, marked public domain.

The example page lays these panels out with mangacn's Page model and letters the dialogue again as real text,
transcribed from the original balloons.
